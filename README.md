# GT Electronics Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project involves analyzing 10,000+ sales records for GT Electronics using Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. It combines an interactive Excel dashboard with a presentation highlighting key business insights, sales trends, and recommendations.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights to understand business performance and identify potential areas for improvement.

![GT Electronics Sales Dashboard](dashboard_preview.png)

## Key Analysis

- **Revenue Analysis:** Evaluated overall revenue performance and monthly growth trends.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Examined profit margins and operating profit across months.
- **Product Analysis:** Compared revenue contributions across product categories.
- **Regional Analysis:** Evaluated sales performance across different regions.
- **Sales Representative Analysis:** Compared individual sales contributions and performance.
- **Monthly Trends:** Identified seasonal patterns and revenue fluctuations.

## Key Insights

- Gaming PCs emerged as the leading product category in revenue contribution.
- The West region recorded the highest revenue among regions.
- Profit margins fluctuated despite overall revenue growth.
- October showed a notable seasonal sales peak.
- Sales contributions varied significantly across representatives.
